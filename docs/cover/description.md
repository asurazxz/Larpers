# Larpers: Kalimantan SAF network planner

**Tagline:** A solver picks which biomass to collect, where to build, and what it costs to fuel four Kalimantan airports with SAF.

## Description (496 characters)

Larpers plans Kalimantan's sustainable aviation fuel supply chain. A MILP solver (HiGHS) picks which of 80 biomass sites to collect, where to open depots and refineries, and how fuel reaches four airports needing 300 kt each, minimising annualised CapEx, OpEx and transport on the organisers' parameters. Base case: 6 refineries, 24 depots, $894M a year, optimal (0% gap) and 39% cheaper than a greedy baseline. An audit checks every result; planners can change inputs and re-solve live on a map.
